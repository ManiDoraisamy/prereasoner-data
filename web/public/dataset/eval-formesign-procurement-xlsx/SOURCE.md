# Formatted procurement workbook

Somerset Council Contract Transparency Q1 2026-2027. Original unmodified .xlsx,
downloaded 2026-09-12 from the public download link on:
https://www.somerset.gov.uk/finance-performance-and-legal/contracts-register/

Public file: https://somersetcc.sharepoint.com/:x:/s/SCCPublic/IQDjlWeKJPbqRriBw-QqBH0HASSu9vH5y-JJ16cGOySZa_w?download=1
Licence: Open Government Licence v3.0, as stated on the source page, except where
otherwise stated. Attribution: Somerset Council.

Two formatted worksheets: Contracts Register (header row 2, 1007 records) and
Purchase orders over £5000 (1168 records). Date cells, wrapped descriptions,
spaces in column names and a leading blank row are retained in the original.
Gold purchase-order values use the latter sheet F2:F1169, summed in pennies.
Contract rows can repeat a Number for multiple suppliers: do not silently
deduplicate them or assume a one-to-one contract-to-order relationship.

Use case: Formesign supplier/contract registers and Neartail procurement totals.
Evaluation-only fixture; not an example or training source. Absence from base-model
pretraining is not established.
