register 2026, downloaded 2026-09-12 without alteration.
Source: https://www.haileandwiltonpc.co.uk/documents/

formatted dates/costs, and two empty worksheets. Expected purchase costs are
491.38 + 847.98 + 170 = 1509.36. Disposal status is blank for two records; blanks
must not turn into an invented Active status. No formulas or macros are executed.

examples. Base-model pretraining exclusion is not established. Original publisher
