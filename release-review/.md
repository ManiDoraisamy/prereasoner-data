## 2. Correctness findings

### B01 — P1: parsing destroys text identity before column roles are known

**Evidence: reproduced.**
Locations: [tables.py:152](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/tables.py:152), [tables.py:168](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/tables.py:168), [tables.py:175](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/tables.py:175); table_from_rows uses the same typing path.

Strings are stripped, unquoted again after CSV decoding, and eagerly converted to numbers. The conversion happens per cell before identifier/measure roles are established.

~~~text
CSV: code,amount / 001,10 / 1,20
Parsed rows: [[1, 10], [1, 20]]

CSV label values: "'Paris'" and Paris
Parsed rows: [["Paris"], ["Paris"]]
~~~

The first example merges distinct identifiers; the second removes literal quote characters already preserved by CSV decoding. Distinct counts, groups, joins, and displayed evidence can change. Saved references share the same transformation.

**Fix:** preserve decoded source scalars in the existing normalization owner. Infer numeric use at a column/role boundary while retaining exact identifier/ambiguous text. Remove unconditional second unquoting of data cells. Represent required numeric interpretation through existing typed semantics rather than overwriting the sole source value.

**Acceptance:** CSV and structured-reference tests for 001 versus 1, postal/account codes, literal quotes, significant spaces, and ordinary measures. Assert exact values, distinct counts, join cardinality, hashes, and readouts. Preserve exact decimal calculations.

### B02 — P1: decimal-comma values are silently multiplied

**Evidence: reproduced.**
Location: [numeric.py:63](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/numeric.py:63).

parse_decimal removes every comma without validating a grouping pattern or source locale. Quoted CSV values "1,50" and "12,34" become 150 and 1234.

**Impact:** incorrect monetary totals and thresholds can pass later arithmetic/parity checks because the operand has already changed.

**Fix:** distinguish grouping from decimal separators through explicit source metadata or a consistent, unambiguous column format. Preserve or reject ambiguity instead of stripping it. Extend the one numeric/source contract rather than introducing separate upload/reference/execution parsers.

**Acceptance:** 1,234; 1,50; 1.234,56; 1,234.56; negatives; nonbreaking-space separators; mixed-format columns. An ambiguous input must not silently produce a 100-fold error. Compare independently derived dataset expectations.

### B03 — P1: numeric equality is not proved by the query contract

**Evidence: reproduced with a constructed typed AST.**
Locations: [query_contract.py:342](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/query_contract.py:342), [query_contract.py:568](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/query_contract.py:568).

Numeric proof runs only when a requested comparison is not equality. The proof helper also skips equality.

~~~text
Question: orders where Amount equals 10
Candidate: SELECT Amount FROM orders WHERE Amount > 10
constraint_violations: ()
coverage.complete: True
~~~

This establishes false acceptance by the guard, not that the example wins every normal search. The guard also protects the original request after a wording rewrite.

**Fix:** prove explicit equality with matching operand, value, scope, and mandatory-predicate semantics. Distinguish filters from ranking cutoffs, dates, and numeric identifiers.

**Acceptance:** equality, reversed inequality, wrong number/column, missing predicate, equality under a non-mandatory OR, grouped HAVING, and numbers inside field names. Run query-contract/AST regressions and fresh served whole_db evaluation.

### B04 — P1: wrong-field exclusion satisfies the negation guard

**Evidence: reproduced with a constructed typed AST.**
Location: [query_contract.py:364](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/query_contract.py:364).

The guard accepts any mandatory negative comparison or negated membership/existence predicate. It does not bind exclusion to the requested field/value.

~~~text
Question: orders not Done in France
Candidate: WHERE status = 'Done' AND country != 'France'
constraint_violations: ()
coverage.complete: True
~~~

The country is negated instead of the status, reversing the subset while coverage reports completeness.

**Fix:** bind polarity to a typed requested constraint and subject/value. Preserve legitimate literals such as "Not Started" and "No"; a blanket lexical exclusion would regress them.

**Acceptance:** multiple positive/negative filters, two exclusions, literals containing exclusion words, NOT IN/NOT EXISTS, null exclusions, and swapped-field negation. Another valid exclusion must not certify the wrong field.

### B06 — P1: literal substring requests become wildcards or another punctuated value

**Evidence: reproduced through SQLSearcher.search and the existing LIKE operator.**
Locations: [sql_search.py:978](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/sql_search.py:978), [sql_search.py:1906](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/sql_search.py:1906), [sql_search.py:1922](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/sql_search.py:1922), [operators.py:297](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/deterministic/operators.py:297).

Substring expansion constructs LIKE '%text%' without escaping literal wildcard characters. Coverage recognizes the same pattern and misses the semantic change.

~~~text
Values: a_b, acb, a%b, axyb
Question: names containing 'a_b'
Generated predicate: LOWER(name) LIKE '%a_b%'
Existing LIKE operator: acb matches

~~~

The second case also reveals punctuation-insensitive value selection: a requested literal percent string can be replaced by an underscore string.

**Fix:** retain exact quoted substring text and distinguish literal containment from intentional wildcard patterns in the typed AST. Implement escape semantics consistently in SQL rendering, Python emission, and coverage.

**Acceptance:** literal percent, underscore, backslash, apostrophes, Unicode, punctuation-only differences, multiple rows, and ordinary substrings. Execute both emitters; string-rendering assertions alone are insufficient.

### B07 — P1: normalized FK discovery disagrees with raw join equality

**Evidence: reproduced through discover_fks and an executed rendered join.**
Locations: [relations.py:76](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/relations.py:76), [relations.py:109](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/relations.py:109), [sql_schema.py:277](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/sql_schema.py:277), [sql_grounding.py:219](C:/work/prereasoner-data/engine/sql_grounding.py:219).

Discovery compares stripped/lowercased values; join construction executes equality on original columns. Grounding also folds case/spacing.

~~~text
Orders keys: ABC, ABC, DEF; amounts: 10, 20, 30
Customers keys: abc, def
Discovered FK: confidence 1.0, inclusion 1.0
Join: orders.customer_id = customers.customer_id

~~~

The defect is the mismatch between claimed inclusion and execution, regardless of the ultimate product policy for case-sensitive identifiers.

**Fix:** discovery and grounding must use the equality semantics of the resulting AST. Reject normalized-only relations or represent justified normalization explicitly in the join and both emitters. Do not globally lowercase user keys. Private-reference selection already has an exact execution-value guard; align the semantic contract.

**Acceptance:** case/space differences, numeric/text keys, truly distinct case-sensitive IDs, duplicate parents, composite keys. Assert detected edges and executed cardinality/results.

### B08 — P1: stale FX history gets validity relative to rebuild time

**Evidence: reproduced through build_rows/_row_stream.**
Location: [build_exchange_rate.py:70](C:/work/prereasoner-data/db/sync/build_exchange_rate.py:70), particularly lines 91–99.

The seven-day horizon is today + seven days. A series present on the final history date is considered active until that horizon even when its final observation is old.

~~~text

Rebuild date: 2026-02-01
Generated USD validity includes 2026-02-08
updated_at remains 2026-01-01

~~~

A stale-feed rebuild therefore makes an old foreign rate available for current-date conversion beyond the stated carry bound.

**Fix:** constrain each series by its latest genuine observation and carry policy, including historical gaps. Separate EUR/EUR identity from unsupported foreign-rate carry. A maintenance run must not extend validity merely by running.

**Acceptance:** weekends, long holidays, outages, withdrawn currencies, missing pair legs, historical gaps, and rebuilding unchanged old history later. No unsupported non-identity rate outside the carry window.
